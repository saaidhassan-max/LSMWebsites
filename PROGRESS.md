Per piece: `simulate()` integrates velocity at 120Hz with gravity (1700–2300 px/s²) and LINEAR AIR DRAG (`v -= drag*v*dt`, drag 2.4–4.2/s) applied to BOTH axes, plus a sinusoidal side-to-side flutter (amplitude 5–22px, freq 3.5–9 rad/s, random phase) and continuous spin (200–720°, random direction). The resulting trajectory is sampled to 26 points and played via the **Web Animations API** (`element.animate(frames, …)`, `fill: 'both'`, `easing: 'linear'` — linear is REQUIRED, the physics is already baked into the sampled point spacing; any other easing would double-apply an ease).
